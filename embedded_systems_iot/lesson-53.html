Practical Lab

  1. Estimate current in active, radio, and sleep states; calculate weighted average current for a duty cycle; compare predicted runtime with a documented battery test plan.

Common Pitfall

Calculating runtime from sleep current alone and ignoring radio activity, retries, regulators, and battery conditions.

Check Your Understanding

1. How estimate average current across states?

Multiply each state's current by its time fraction and sum contributions.

2. What can ruin sleep current?

Unexpected wakeups, active peripherals, timers, logging, or network activity.

3. What should a battery test record?

Workload, current/voltage, temperature, battery details, duration, and instrument setup.

  • Calculate weighted average current.
