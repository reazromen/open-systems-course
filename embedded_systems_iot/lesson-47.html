Practical Lab

  1. Inspect TLS trust anchors, hostname validation, and time requirements; test trusted and controlled certificate/hostname failures in development without disabling verification.

Common Pitfall

Disabling certificate verification to make a connection succeed defeats peer authentication.

Check Your Understanding

1. What does certificate validation establish?

That the peer identity chains to trusted material and matches the expected identity.

2. Why is encryption alone insufficient?

Traffic may be encrypted to an impostor if the peer is not authenticated.

3. Why does time matter?

Certificate validity checks commonly depend on trustworthy time.

Key Takeaways

  • Explain encryption versus authentication.
