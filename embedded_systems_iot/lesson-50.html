Practical Lab

  1. Specify a BLE or HTTP operation with authentication, limits, timeouts, and errors; test malformed and repeated requests; document what unauthorized nearby clients can do.

Common Pitfall

Assuming a local radio or LAN interface is trusted because it is nearby.

Check Your Understanding

1. When is BLE useful?

Nearby commissioning or low-power local interactions.

2. What should an HTTP API specify?

Authentication, schema, timeouts, errors, and repeated-request behavior.

3. Why is local access a security boundary?

Nearby clients may still be malicious or unauthorized.
