Practical Lab

  1. Design telemetry, command, and reported-state topics; document QoS, retained policy, sessions, duplicate handling, and command IDs; test reconnect and repeated delivery.

Common Pitfall

Assuming QoS 1 means a command executes exactly once, or treating retained state as an event log.

Check Your Understanding

1. What does QoS 1 mean?

At-least-once delivery, so duplicates are possible.

2. Why make commands idempotent?

Retries should not cause unintended repeated effects.

3. What is a retained message?

The broker's stored latest message for a topic, not complete history.

  • Choose topic structure.
